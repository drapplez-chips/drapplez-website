A Guide to Our Shimla Apples

“Don’t judge a fruit by its look 🍎. Raw and natural produce may not always appear shiny, spotless, or perfect — but that’s the beauty of it.”

A Note on Apple Colour

An apple's bright red blush is like a suntan, developing where skin meets sunlight. Greener spots were simply shaded by a leaf. This natural color variation is a sign of authentic ripening and doesn't affect the sweet, crisp taste inside.

Understanding "Touching"

"Touching" describes minor, invisible bruising that can occur during handling. While we inspect every apple carefully, the delicate nature of fresh fruit makes it impossible to eliminate completely. We appreciate your understanding!

Marks: Hail Kisses

The small brown spots on some apples are natural scars from hailstorms, which we call "hail kisses." The apple has already healed itself, and these purely cosmetic marks don't affect the delicious, crisp fruit underneath.
